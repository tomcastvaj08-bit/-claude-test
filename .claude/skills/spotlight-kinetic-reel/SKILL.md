# Spotlight kinetic reel ("Let's make it pay.")

The reel is a 1080×1920, 30 fps, 223-frame (7.43 s) vertical video. **Only the character (the person and the chair) is taken from the source footage.** The pipeline cuts the character out and paints the studio procedurally. All typography, graphics and sound are generated by the scripts here. The face in the source is already pixelated, and it stays that way.

```
source.mp4 ─► frames ─► BiRefNet matte ─► cutouts (person + chair)
                     └► ECC camera track ─► world→screen camera per frame
procedural studio (spotlight cone, floor pool) + canvas graphics in WORLD space
character cutout in SCREEN space (camera already baked into the footage)
procedural sound design locked to frame numbers ─► ffmpeg (grain, x264, aac)
```

## Run it

```bash
pip install rembg onnxruntime opencv-python-headless numpy pillow
python3 -c "from rembg import new_session; new_session('birefnet-portrait')"   # model download
bash .claude/skills/spotlight-kinetic-reel/scripts/build.sh SOURCE.mp4 /path/to/workdir OUT.mp4
```

The steps are resumable. Segmentation is the slow part: about 15–25 s per frame on 4 CPU cores, roughly 1 h for 182 frames. Run it in the background. To iterate on graphics only, edit `template/composition.html`, copy it to `WORK/work/`, and render a few frames: `node scripts/render_frames.mjs WORK/work WORK/render 88 104 2`. Before re-rendering everything, compare against the original side by side, using a contact sheet of the original frame above the rendered one.

**Gotchas**
- rembg's own session grows past 14 GB and gets OOM-killed. `segment_character.py` runs onnxruntime with the memory arena turned off, which uses about 2 GB.
- Chromium blocks `fetch()` on `file://`. `render_frames.mjs` serves the work dir over a tiny local HTTP server.
- Hugging Face is blocked in some sandboxes, so faster-whisper can't load. The rembg models come from GitHub releases and do work.
- Fonts are bundled in `assets/fonts`: Inter Display 400/500/700 and Italianno, both OFL. Don't rely on system fonts.

## Layering (spotlight shots)

1. Procedural background (`make_background.py`), in world space: wall #050505–#0c0c0c with faint vertical panel stripes, a spotlight cone from the lamp at top centre, a horizon at y≈925, and a floor light pool (an ellipse at 360,1012 with rx 420 and ry 72) plus a vignette.
2. Glass card / squircle, in world space.
3. Texts that sit **behind the head**, in world space. The head overlaps "price.", "value." and "pay.", which is the whole look.
4. Character shadow: the silhouette from the matte, flipped, sheared (1.25, 0, 0.35, −0.3), blurred and laid at the feet line (world y 995).
5. Character cutout, in screen space.
6. Foreground in world space: crosses, plane, badge. Comet and exit plane are in screen space.
7. Global defocus on blurry camera frames (F1–7, F136–138, F180–189), on the BG and FG layers only. The footage already carries its own blur.

World space is source frame 60 (720×1280). The camera from `track_camera.py` is `screen = s·world + t`. The texts and the card move with the camera push-in at F84–F104, so they must live in world space.

## Timeline (source frame F, 30 fps, F1 = 0.00 s)

**Shot A, F1–138 (footage): "You're not competing on price." → "You're competing on value."**
- F1–10: footage zooms out with motion blur (camera s 2.2 → 1.15). The card is already there at F3 (fade F1.2–3.8, scale 1.1→1). Crosses fly in: the big one comes from the right, the small ones scale-spin in.
- F3–27: a white comet (tapered stroke, glow) flies right→left at y≈260 and **writes "You're not" right-to-left**. Each letter pops (scale 1.5→1, blur→0). The comet curls around the small cross and dies at F27. A second short streak exits left at F21–28.
- F7.5–15: five yellow rounded squares (48 px, gap 5.75) pop in dim (olive) and brighten about 2 frames later. A brightness wave runs through them (F16–22), then they shrink out left→right (F22–30).
- F26–31.5: "price." slides in from the left with a horizontal smear (−230 px, easeOutCubic).
- F27.5–35: "competing" is hand-written left→right (a clip reveal with a pen-tip bloom). "on" fades in yellow → white (F32.5–41).
- Crosses snap-rotate 45° (X ↔ +) at F11, 26, 50, 65 and 83. The small crosses lag by a few frames.
- F35–80: a white paper-plane cursor glides in from the right, arcs over the top-right and docks on the right end of the pill (F80). The pill shortens (F80–86).
- F84–104: the camera pushes in (comes from the footage). Riser → click (F88).
- F88–104: **card collapse**. The card shrinks into a 103×77 blob (F96), then springs into the squircle. The plane becomes the verified badge (F96–99.5, easeOutBack). A second plane exits left (F98–107).
- F91–97: band-sliced glitch from "You're not" to "You're". "price." shrinks out (F92–96.5). "value." pops in (scale 1.38→1, blur 7→0, F96.5–101.5).
- F95–121: a yellow hand-drawn wobbly ring draws on around the statement, dims to olive by F102, keeps expanding and fades out by F121.
- F122–129 and F125–135: "You're" then "competing on" slide out to the right (accelerating, +430 px), tinting yellow with a glow and a light smear. "value." stays.
- F135.5–138: the badge collapses into a white dot. Global defocus. Cut.

**Shot B, F139–179 (pure graphics, screen space): "Does your content / SHOW / it?"**
- Black, with a soft lighter field around (310,600) and a faint dot grid (pitch 160.5 × 133.7).
- The text group whips in from the left (−300 → 0 px, smear and blur) and drifts +8 px. Two thick white corner arcs (r 91, centres at (345±185, 590∓65)) draw on, then rotate slowly.
- F174–179: scale to 0.9, blur and glow, then cut.

**Shot C, F180–223 (footage): "Let's make it / pay."**
- The footage zoom-out repeats the opening (camera F180+k ≈ F1+k).
- "Let's make it" settles from above (F181–193). "pay." fades in at F183. The whole group scales 1.32→1 and de-blurs (F181–197).
- Three hand-drawn outline bubbles (r 21/15/8.5) fly in from the right and drift slowly to the upper-left of the text, settling at F219: (178,325), (220,287), (270,260).

## Sound map (`sound_design.py`, all procedural)

There is a dark pad bed (A, 55–220 Hz, low-passed) plus air noise. Events, by frame:
- F1: zoom whoosh and soft kick. F2: comet whoosh. F7.5–14: loader clicks and blips. F11–16: typing ticks. F22–28: descending blips.
- F19: build. F27.5: "price." impact. F28: pen scribble. F33: sparkle. F35 and F60: plane glides.
- F73: riser. F80 and F88: clicks. F91: glitch. F92: collapse whoosh. F96.5: badge pop. **F97: "value." drop (sub impact)**. F95: ring swish.
- F121 and F126: slide-out whooshes. F135: dot blip. F132: whip into "show". F139: kick and shimmer. F157 and F160: ticks.
- F168: build to the cut. **F180: impact**. F186–190: bubble pops.

## Reusing it with new copy or a new person

- **Copy:** pass `window.CFG = { text: { a1, a1b, script, on, w1, w2, b1, b2, b3, c1, c2 } }`, or edit `TXT` at the top of `composition.html`. The big words and "pay." centre themselves. Long words may need `CFG.bigSize`, `CFG.showSize` or `CFG.paySize`.
- **New footage of a seated person:** keep the shot lengths (A 138 frames, B 41, C 44), or retime the `P(F, a, b)` windows. Re-run the camera track with a reference frame where the camera is at rest, and re-check the feet line (world y 995) and the shoulder line used to key out leaked graphics in `prepare_plates.py`.
- Always QA against the source with original-over-render contact sheets at the beats listed above.
