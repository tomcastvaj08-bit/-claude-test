# Sound & sync

- Captions follow the **voice**, not a fixed rate. Get word timestamps (Whisper /
  ElevenLabs transcription with word timings). Each word appears on its onset frame
  (round down). Hero words may land **1 f early** — it reads as "on the beat".
- If no voice: 150–170 wpm pacing → a word every 6 f, hero words hold +8 f.
- SFX map (keep subtle, −18 to −12 LUFS under voice):
  - Pop (A1) small words: none or soft tick.
  - Slam (A3) / hero condensed: low thump + short riser end.
  - Skew-slide (A4): short whoosh starting 2 f before.
  - Typewriter (A6): soft key clicks 1 per 2 chars.
  - Binary decode (A8): digital glitch blip.
  - Whip-flash (T1): whoosh f4, hit f10.
  - Pixel blocks (T2): granular digital "crunch", 200 ms.
  - Counter (A15): ticking that speeds up then a ding on final value.
  - Card fly-in (A13): paper swish; pin drop: tiny click.
- Music: lo-fi/cinematic bed, duck −8 dB under voice; cut music to silence for 0.5–1 s
  before a key statement, hit back on the hero word.
- Section changes land on a musical downbeat if music is used.
